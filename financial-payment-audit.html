Financial & Payment Audit

Three-way reconciliation of daily workshop cash, UPI QR payments, card POS terminals, and bank deposits against DMS invoices, customer debtor ledgers, and credit notes.

Practice Overview

Closing the Loop Between Invoicing and Bank Realization

In busy service dealerships handling 40 to 100 customer settlements per day across multiple cashiers and payment channels (Cash, dynamic UPI, payment gateways, credit cards, NEFT/RTGS, and fleet credit accounts), payment reconciliation gaps inevitably arise. Cash can be delayed in deposit, credit card fee deductions miscalculated, and customer short-payments swept into suspense accounts.

Our Financial & Payment Audit conducts rigorous three-way matching between DMS tax invoices, payment receipts, cashier day books, and official bank statements. We eliminate unmatched receipts, scrutinize customer debtor ageing, verify refund authorizations, and ensure every rupee billed is physically accounted for in your corporate bank account.

Strategic Imperative

Why Dealerships Need This Audit

Daily collections involve cash, credit card POS, dynamic UPI QR codes, insurance NEFTs, and customer credit ledger accounts. Cash delay deposits, uncleared POS settlements, unallocated customer advances, and uncollected insurance surveyor excess deductions silently drain liquidity.

Common Ground Blind Spots Uncovered:
  • Cash collected from customers deposited with 3 to 7-day delays (cash-float skimming).
  • Credit card POS settlement fees miscalculated or uncleared by merchant banks.
  • Advance booking payments left unallocated in suspense ledgers.
  • Uncollected customer excess and compulsory insurance deductibles on claim jobs.
Comprehensive Scope

What We Audit

  • Cash & UPI Reconciliation: Comparing cashier daily collections against actual bank deposit slips and UPI gateway logs.
  • Bank Statement vs DMS Match: Transaction-by-transaction verification confirming receipts deposited into authorized accounts.
  • Payment Mode Discrepancies: Identifying invoices marked "Paid by Card/UPI" in DMS with missing gateway settlement records.
  • Outstanding Debtors Ageing: Line-level scrutiny of corporate, insurance, and retail customer receivables (>30, >60, >90 days).
  • Customer Refunds & Reversals: Auditing justification, management approvals, and banking proof for all processed refunds.
  • Credit & Debit Note Controls: Validating credit notes issued against service invoices to ensure no unauthorized revenue reversal.
  • Short-Payment Identification: Catching rounding-off abuses and customer balance shortfalls left uncollected at delivery.
  • Unmatched Advance Deposits: Reconciling customer repair advances against final invoices to ensure accurate adjustment.
Risk Exposure

Common Cashier & Financial Vulnerabilities

Financial Control Breakdowns Discovered in Audits:

  • Teeming and Lading (Cash Rolling): Cash collected today held by cashier or advisor and deposited days later using subsequent customer cash.
  • Ghost UPI Entries: Cashiers entering fabricated UPI transaction reference numbers (UTR) to close repair orders without actual bank credit.
  • Aged Debtor Ballooning: Retail customer balances left open as "Receivables" after vehicle delivery, gradually turning into bad debt write-offs.
  • Fictitious Credit Notes: Credit memos created at month-end to wipe out customer outstanding balances without General Manager authorization.

Procedure

Our Financial Audit Procedure

1
Automated 3-Way Match

Ingestion of DMS billing register, cashier receipt register, and official bank statement into specialized reconciliation scripts.

2
Deposit Delay Testing

Computing calendar day delay between cash receipt generation and physical cash deposit credit in bank to identify cash rolling.

3
UTR Verification

Cross-verifying unique transaction references for all digital UPI/NEFT transactions against live bank clearing statements.

4
Debtor Scrutiny

Deep dive into individual debtor ledgers, identifying vehicles delivered without payment and analyzing disputed balances.

5
Credit Note Validation

Checking supporting documents, original invoices, and approval signatures for 100% of issued credit notes.
