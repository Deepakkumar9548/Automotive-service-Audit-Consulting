Customer Experience Audit

Independent verification of genuine customer satisfaction (CSI & NPS), post-service feedback authenticity, complaint resolution TAT, and customer retention dynamics.

Practice Overview

Unfiltered Customer Reality vs Manufactured Feedback

In dealership workshops, staff incentives and OEM quarterly bonuses are tied heavily to Customer Satisfaction Index (CSI) and Net Promoter Scores (NPS). Unfortunately, this creates strong incentives for internal teams to "manage" feedback—such as logging employee phone numbers as customer contacts, coaching customers, or marking unresolved complaints as "satisfied" without actual rectification.

Our Customer Experience Audit provides Dealer Principals with the unvarnished truth. We audit the complete feedback pipeline: validating contact data authenticity, reviewing customer complaint closure Turnaround Time (TAT), cross-referencing repeat repair visits, and measuring true customer retention across vehicle vintages.

Strategic Imperative

Why Dealerships Need This Audit

Dissatisfied service customers do not return. Process failures—such as advisor over-promising delivery times, billing surprises without prior estimate approval, dirty car interiors, and ignored complaints—directly crash customer retention and OEM CSI scores.

Common Ground Blind Spots Uncovered:
  • Repairs conducted without written or digital customer estimate approvals.
  • Vehicles delivered past promised delivery times without prior notification.
  • Repeat repair jobs due to incomplete first-time problem diagnosis.
  • Post-service customer follow-up calls unmade or fabricated by telecallers.
Comprehensive Scope

What We Audit

  • NPS & CSI Authenticity: Statistical validation of satisfaction ratings against independent sample re-calls.
  • Customer Contact Data Integrity: Detection of duplicate mobile numbers, staff numbers, and invalid customer emails.
  • Post-Service Follow-up (PSF): Verification of call logs, actual customer reach rates, and negative feedback capture.
  • Complaint Escalation & Closure TAT: Tracking time from customer grievance logging to genuine technical resolution.
  • Repeat Repair Analysis: Correlation between unresolved complaints and vehicles returning within 30 days.
  • Customer Retention & Churn: Retention tracking across 1st Year, 2nd Year, 3rd Year, and post-warranty cohorts.
  • Promised Delivery vs Actual Handover: Timeliness compliance comparing promised ready time with gatepass generation.
  • Service Explanation & Estimate Transparency: Review of invoice explanation records and customer signature logs.
Risk Exposure

Common Customer Experience Vulnerabilities

Manufactured Feedback & Retention Risks:

  • Contact Masking: Service advisors entering their own or relatives' phone numbers on troublesome repair orders to intercept OEM feedback calls.
  • Premature Complaint Closure: Customer complaints marked as "Resolved" in CRM within hours, while the customer is still waiting for a callback or parts replacement.
  • Steep Post-Warranty Drop-off: Severe drop in retention after Year 3 (often losing >60% of vehicle owners to independent garages) due to perceived overcharging.
  • Chronic Repeat Failures: Unresolved noises, AC cooling complaints, or electrical glitches leading to customer acrimony and brand erosion.

Procedure

Our Customer Experience Audit Procedure

1
Mobile Number De-Duplication

Run algorithmic scripts on customer master ledgers to detect repeated phone numbers and cross-match with staff directory.

2
Independent Sample Validation

Conduct independent blind audit calls to a representative sample of customers who serviced their cars during the month to compare actual satisfaction against CRM records.

3
Complaint Lifecycle Audit

Audit 100% of escalated complaints (OEM Tier-1 grievances, legal notices, and social media escalations) tracking closure truth and customer satisfaction.

4
Repeat Visit Diagnostic

Trace all vehicles returning within 45 days, correlating the technical repair with previous service advisor and technician IDs.

5
Retention Cohort Analysis

Track dealership active service base retention across vehicle models and ages to identify lost customer segments.
