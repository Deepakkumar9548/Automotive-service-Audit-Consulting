Privacy & Dealership Data Policy

Institutional confidentiality protocols safeguarding client data, DMS transactional records, financial ledgers, and customer information.

Last Updated: November 2026 | Effective for all Automotive Audit & Consulting Engagements

1. Principle of Total Client Confidentiality

As an independent automotive after-sales audit and consulting firm, we recognize that our clients entrust us with sensitive commercial, financial, and operational records. We operate under strict professional confidentiality rules. All information received during preliminary discussions or formal audit execution is treated as strictly proprietary and confidential.

2. Scope of Data Ingestion

In performing our audits, we collect and process only data strictly necessary for fulfilling the agreed audit scope:

  • DMS Transaction Logs (Job Cards, Tax Invoices, Issue Slips, Pre-Invoices).
  • Security Gate Records (Gate-In, Gate-Out, and Road Test Logs).
  • Inventory Records (Stock ledgers, GRNs, Scrap logs, Stock adjustments).
  • Accounting Records (Cashier receipt registers, bank statements, debtor ledgers).
  • OEM Portal Reports (Warranty claims, credit notes, and chargeback debit notes).

3. Prohibition of Third-Party Monetization or Disclosure

We strictly maintain a zero-monetization policy:

  • We never sell, lease, monetize, or barter dealership operational data, pricing matrices, customer contacts, or financial figures.
  • We never share client data with automobile manufacturers (OEMs), competitors, or third-party suppliers without explicit, written authorization from the Dealer Principal.
  • Audit findings and exception registers belong solely to the client dealership management.

4. Technical Security & Access Controls

All electronic data files provided via secure SFTP or encrypted cloud storage are protected by enterprise-grade safeguards:

  • AES-256 bit encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.
  • Role-based access control: only auditors assigned to the specific dealership engagement are granted file access.
  • Watermarked working papers and non-exportable audit staging databases.

5. Data Retention & Secure Purging

Upon presentation of the Final Audit Report and completion of the mandatory 60-day remediation follow-up window, all raw transactional datasets, customer phone numbers, and cashier registers are securely purged from our active audit servers in accordance with DoD 5220.22-M sanitization standards. Only final signed PDF audit deliverables and workpaper indexes are retained for legal record-keeping.
