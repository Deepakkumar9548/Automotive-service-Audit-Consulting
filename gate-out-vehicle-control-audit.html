Gate Out & Vehicle Control Audit

Critical physical security reconciliation linking physical gatepass registers to finalized DMS invoices, eliminating unverified departures and unauthorized deliveries.

Practice Overview

The Ultimate Control Point: Ensuring Zero Unbilled Exits

The dealership workshop gate is the final physical barrier safeguarding your business assets. If a customer vehicle exits the facility before the tax invoice is generated, approved, and paid in full, the dealership immediately assumes uncollectible credit risk. In practice, manual handwritten gatepasses, friendly staff overrides, and late-night customer releases routinely compromise security protocols.

Our Gate Out & Vehicle Control Audit (GOR Reconciliation) cross-reconciles 100% of physical security gate-out logs against digital DMS audit records. We identify vehicles dispatched without audit verification, catch vehicles leaving without any gate register entry, investigate off-hours/late-night departures, and eliminate handwritten manual pass bypasses.

Strategic Imperative

Why Dealerships Need This Audit

Security gate control is the final physical barrier protecting dealership revenue. Vehicles exiting without paid invoices, manual handwritten gate passes issued on verbal promises, and uninspected customer loaner vehicles create severe financial loss and legal liability.

Common Ground Blind Spots Uncovered:
  • Vehicles driven out on handwritten paper passes before final invoice settlement.
  • Gate entry registers missing odometer and fuel level readings.
  • Demo and loaner vehicles used for personal staff transport without trip authorization.
  • Customer vehicles released without returning replaced old parts in boot.
Comprehensive Scope

What We Audit

  • Job Card vs Gate Out Register: 1:1 cross-reconciliation linking every security gate departure to a closed, fully paid RO.
  • Delivery Without Billing Checks: Identifying physical gate departures where no valid final tax invoice was generated.
  • Unregistered Gate Departures: Invoiced vehicles marked delivered in DMS that have zero corresponding entry in security registers.
  • Manual Gatepass Disallowance: Identifying bypass of digital QR gatepasses through handwritten paper slips.
  • Late-Night & Off-Hours Exits: Analysis of vehicles exiting after 8:00 PM or on Sundays without executive management intimation.
  • Free Service & PDI Vehicle Movement: Gate controls over new car pre-delivery inspection (PDI) moves and test-drive fleet.
  • Inter-Branch Vehicle Movement: Tracking vehicles shuttling between main workshop and satellite bodyshops or paint booths.
  • Gatepass Documentation Archive: Physical audit of security gatepass counterfoils, customer sign-offs, and driver identities.
Risk Exposure

Critical Gate Control Vulnerabilities

High-Risk Physical Security Gaps Found in Dealerships:

  • Dispatches Without Audit Verification: In real dealership audits, we frequently find 60+ vehicles released on handwritten slips where security has no record of invoice payment.
  • Missing Security Logs: 20–30 vehicles per month leaving workshop premises without security guard logging vehicle registration, driver, or time.
  • Late-Night Undocumented Releases: High-value customer cars delivered after 9:00 PM without GM/Director WhatsApp or email approval, risking stolen parts or uncollected balances.
  • Road-Test Gatepass Abuse: Vehicles exiting on "Road Test" or "Washing" gatepasses that never return or are returned days later.

Procedure

Our GOR Audit Procedure

1
Full Month Dataset Matching

Ingest physical gate-out log lines and DMS audited records into matching engines using `Location + Vehicle Number` compound keys.

2
Exception Classification

Segregate unmatched records into: (a) Gate-Out verified, (b) Dispatch without audit verification, and (c) Audit records with missing gate entry.

3
Inter-Branch Resolution

Check whether unmatched vehicle registrations at Branch A were serviced or invoiced at Branch B (inter-branch dispatch reconciliation).

4
Manual Gate Pass Forensic Audit

Inspect physical serial numbers of manual gatepass counterfoils to confirm every manual exit had written General Manager sanction.

5
Off-Hours Analysis

Flag all vehicle departures occurring between 8:00 PM and 7:00 AM, verifying intimation logs with executive management.
