Roadside Assistance (RSA) Audit

Independent verification of RSA policy enrolments, third-party towing vendor bills, OEM reimbursement receivables, claim reconciliations, and expense-to-revenue ratios.

Practice Overview

Protecting Dealership Margins Across Roadside Assistance & Breakdown Logistics

Roadside Assistance (RSA) is a critical customer retention touchpoint and ancillary revenue stream for automotive dealerships. However, because RSA transactions involve complex interactions between dealer service advisors, third-party towing operators, OEM breakdown assistance desks, and insurance underwriters, it is prone to significant cost leakage and unrecovered claims.

Our Roadside Assistance (RSA) Audit delivers a rigorous examination of both the revenue side (enrolments, renewals, package sales) and the operational expense side (breakdown towing invoices, mechanic dispatch charges, and OEM reimbursement claims). We reconcile dispatch logs with workshop Job Cards and accounting ledgers to eliminate phantom vendor billings, recover unbilled claims, and enforce operational governance.

Strategic Imperative

Why Dealerships Need This Audit

RSA operations involve external towing contractors, OEM roadside platforms, and emergency roadside dispatches. Discrepancies abound: towing vendors overbilling mileage, duplicate claims, unregistered customer RSA policies, and delay penalties going unrecovered.

Common Ground Blind Spots Uncovered:
  • Towing vendor invoices inflated beyond actual GPS mileage and agreed contract rates.
  • Duplicate reimbursement claims submitted for the same vehicle breakdown event.
  • Breakdown dispatches provided to non-policy vehicles without collecting customer payment.
  • Reimbursement claims delayed past OEM portal deadlines, resulting in permanent forfeiture.
Scope of Scrutiny

The 12 Core RSA Audit Workstreams

Our engagement covers every phase of the roadside assistance lifecycle from initial policy issuance to vendor payout and OEM claim closure:

1. RSA Registration Verification

Cross-auditing vehicle delivery files, service bookings, and DMS records against active RSA registrations to ensure zero unregistered policies.

2. Free vs Paid RSA Package Checks

Verification of OEM standard warranty RSA entitlements versus dealer-promoted paid RSA extensions to prevent unauthorized complimentary enrolments.

3. RSA Activation & Customer Records

Auditing membership issuance timelines, policy certificate distribution, customer contact validation, and portal synchronization with OEM/third-party RSA providers.

4. Service Request & Dispatch Verification

Scrutiny of breakdown dispatch logs against incoming call recordings, GPS vehicle telemetry, tow truck logs, and physical workshop inbound gate entries.

5. Vendor Invoice Verification

Auditing third-party flatbed and wheel-lift towing agency invoices against approved rate cards, contracted kilometer slabs, waiting charges, and actual GPS routes.

6. OEM Reimbursement Verification

Reconciling eligible warranty breakdown towing claims lodged with the OEM against approved credit notes and settlement remittances.

7. Pending Claims & Aging Analysis

Detailed age-bucket scrutiny (30/60/90+ days) of unbilled and unapproved RSA claims to prevent claim expiry under strict OEM time bars.

8. Duplicate Claim Identification

Algorithmic detection of duplicate claim filings across insurance policy clauses, OEM roadside warranty, and customer direct cash recovery for the same incident.

9. Customer Payment & Co-Pay Verification

Verification of customer co-pay collections for excess mileage beyond allowable coverage limits, ensuring cash receipts are fully posted to dealership accounts.

10. RSA Billing Discrepancy Audits

Detection of tariff rate inflations, incorrect tax categorization, missing toll slips, unapproved overnight storage fees, and non-contractual crane service charges.

11. Supporting Document Verification

Mandatory documentation checks including breakdown incident photos, customer consent signatures, technician breakdown reports, and towing delivery slips.

12. RSA Revenue & Expense P&L Analysis

Net operational profitability modeling per branch, evaluating premium income retained vs total towing and dispatch outlays across multi-location networks.

Vulnerability Scenarios

Where Dealerships Lose Money in Roadside Assistance

Unclaimed OEM Warranty Towing: Dealerships pay local tow-truck operators directly upon vehicle arrival, but workshop service teams frequently forget to file reimbursement claims on the OEM warranty portal before the 30-day window expires.
Inflated Kilometre & Phantom Towing Bills: External towing vendors bill inflated distance slabs (e.g. 60 km billed for a 22 km breakdown) without verified GPS coordinate records or workshop security in-gate confirmation.
Double Recovery / Cash Leakage: Drivers collect excess distance cash directly from stranded car owners, while the vendor simultaneously bills the dealership or insurance company for the full run.
Complimentary RSA Packaging Without Enrollment: Sales teams promise 'Free RSA' to close vehicle sales, but finance desks never upload the enrolment or remit the fee to the central RSA company, leaving customers stranded when breakdowns happen.
Audit Methodology

Four-Phase Triangulation Approach

1

Data Extraction & Three-Way Log Matching

We extract vendor invoices, DMS Job Cards, gate security logs, and OEM breakdown portal records, performing automated data triangulation on VIN and date stamps.

2

Tariff & Rate Card Compliance Audit

Every vendor invoice line item is audited against contracted SLA rate cards, allowable per-kilometer charges, daytime/nighttime differential tariffs, and city municipality restrictions.

3

Claim Recovery & Debit Note Formulation

Identification of recoverable OEM warranty towing receivables and formulation of vendor debit notes for over-billed distance or unsupported towing charges.

4

Policy SOP Standardisation & Governance

Designing strict gate-in tow verification checklists, required GPS photo attachments, and standard operating procedures for real-time OEM claim submission.
