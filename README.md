## 2. All 10 Specialized Audit Services

1. **Workshop Operations Audit (`workshop-operations-audit.html`):** Job card open-to-close velocity, technician bay utilization, flat-rate labour monetization, and ghost job card elimination.
2. **Spare Parts & Inventory Audit (`spare-parts-inventory-audit.html`):** Wall-to-wall physical stock counting vs DMS, negative stock resolution, FIFO binning, and slow-moving parts liquidation.
3. **Service Billing & Revenue Leakage Audit (`service-billing-revenue-leakage-audit.html`):** Unbilled parts recovery, unbilled labour, split repair order detection, manual discount override controls.
4. **Warranty & Insurance Audit (`warranty-insurance-audit.html`):** OEM claim rejections, 30-day resubmission recovery, causal part code validation, quarantine room scrap verification.
5. **Counter Sales Audit (`counter-sales-audit.html`):** Retail OTC walk-in sales, bulk oil meter calibration, battery scrap credit recovery, and cashier daybook settlements.
6. **Roadside Assistance (RSA) Audit (`rsa-audit.html`):** Towing vendor GPS logs, mileage rate slabs, duplicate claim checks, customer entitlement verification, OEM reimbursements.
7. **Customer Experience Audit (`customer-experience-audit.html`):** Digital estimate approval compliance, interior cleanliness, repeat repair analysis, customer retention.
8. **Financial & Payment Audit (`financial-payment-audit.html`):** Cash deposit delay audits, UPI dynamic QR settlements, insurance excess deduction collections, debtors aging.
9. **Gate Out & Vehicle Control Audit (`gate-out-vehicle-control-audit.html`):** Barcode gate pass enforcement, gate-out-without-invoice (GOR) arrest, loaner/demo vehicle controls.
10. **MIS & Performance Analytics (`mis-performance-analytics.html`):** Single-source-of-truth Executive Dashboard, true bay productivity, automated multi-branch benchmarking.

## 5. Contact Form & Lead Routing Setup

The contact form in `contact.html` is fully wired with dual submission options:

1. **Email Route (`btnSubmitEmail`):**
   - Transmits to Netlify Forms and/or Web3Forms API.
   - Awaits provider response (`response.ok` + `data.success === true`).
   - If successful, confirms delivery and provides direct WhatsApp link.
   - If failed, preserves all form inputs and prompts instant WhatsApp connect.
2. **WhatsApp Route (`btnSubmitWhatsApp`):**
   - Validates all 9 fields.
   - Formats a clean, structured 9-point inquiry message with emojis and bold headers.
   - Opens WhatsApp chat directly to the Partner's number.
   - Simultaneously awaits background email dispatch and updates alert status transparently.
