Warranty & Insurance Audit

Forensic examination of OEM warranty claims, chargeback debit notes, aged claim recovery (>180 days), and insurance bodyshop claim reconciliations.

Practice Overview

Safeguarding OEM Recoveries & Eliminating Chargeback Debits

Automotive warranty and insurance receivables represent massive working capital pipelines. However, strict OEM warranty guidelines, 30-day e-invoicing (IRN) mandates, defective part return protocols, and insurance surveyor disallowances lead to tens of lakhs of rupees being routinely deducted or debited back from dealerships.

Our Warranty & Insurance Audit conducts line-by-line reconciliation of all submitted warranty claims against OEM credit and debit notes. We identify the root causes of claim rejections, uncover delayed scrap entries, isolate claims approaching irreversible expiry (>180 days), and reconcile insurance surveyor approval liability against customer collections.

Strategic Imperative

Why Dealerships Need This Audit

Dealerships lose massive sums to OEM warranty claim rejections, missed 30-day resubmission cutoffs, mismatched causal part codes, and failed physical scrap part audits during OEM factory inspections.

Common Ground Blind Spots Uncovered:
  • OEM warranty claims rejected due to documentation errors and never re-filed.
  • Mismatched causal part codes and labor operations triggering claim rejections.
  • Physical failed parts missing from quarantine room during OEM factory audits.
  • Insurance salvage deductions and uncollected customer excess liabilities.
Comprehensive Scope

What We Audit

  • Warranty Claim Verification: 100% check of claims filed vs repair orders, pre-approvals, and customer complaints.
  • OEM Debit Note Analysis: Root cause analysis of all chargeback debit notes issued by the manufacturer.
  • Claims Ageing & Suspense: Ageing classification (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–180, and >180 days) for all uncredited claims.
  • Rejection & Late Submission Review: Audit of claims denied due to exceeding OEM submission deadlines or IRN delays.
  • Extended Warranty & Service Packages: Compliance verification for third-party warranty programs and AMC schemes.
  • Failed Material Return & Scrap Compliance: Verifying defective parts dispatch notes and scrap destruction logs.
  • Insurance Claim Billing: Assessment of supplementary bills, depreciation deductions, and customer compulsory excess.
  • Insurance Settlement Reconciliation: Three-way matching: RO Billing vs Surveyor Work Order vs Insurance Company Payment.
Risk Exposure

Common Warranty & Insurance Vulnerabilities

Critical Chargeback Risks We Regularly Identify:

  • Massive OEM Debit Notes: Lakhs of rupees in chargebacks (e.g. ₹20L+ across 300+ claims) triggered because defective parts were delayed in transit or scrap dates were not entered in the OEM portal.
  • Aged Claims Approaching Write-off: Large volumes of claims stuck in "Suspense / SAP Hold / Returned" older than 180 days that the accounts team has quietly stopped pursuing.
  • Missing Pre-Approvals: High-value non-standard warranty replacements executed without digital OEM Technical Support pre-authorization.
  • Uncollected Insurance Deductibles: Bodyshop vehicles released to owners before customer salvage/depreciation liability was collected.

Procedure

Our Warranty Audit Procedure

1
Claim-to-Credit Note Reconciliation

Compare every claimed invoice line against OEM credit notes to compute exact short-settlement disallowances.

2
Debit Note Categorization

Classify every debit note by root cause: transit delay, scrap entry missing, IRN overdue (>30 days), technical rejection, or incomplete diagnostic photos.

3
Ageing Stratification

Isolate all pending, suspended, and denied claims into ageing buckets, highlighting claims crossing 180 days for emergency management escalation.

4
Physical Tag Verification

Perform a physical inspection of the warranty parts room to confirm tagged defective parts match open claims in the system.

5
Insurance Reco Engine

Match surveyor liability approvals against customer ledger debits to verify zero uncollected deductibles upon vehicle handover.
