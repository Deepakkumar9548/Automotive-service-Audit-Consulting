Tailored Audit Engagement Frameworks

Whether you manage a single flagship dealership or a multi-brand dealer group spanning dozens of outlets, we offer calibrated advisory models engineered for maximum control, non-disruption, and ROI.

Flexible Collaboration

4 Proven Engagement Structures

Every dealership has unique operating scales, risk tolerances, and governance requirements. Choose the advisory cadence best suited to your management objectives.

MODEL 01

Monthly Retainer Audit

Continuous Assurance & Leakage Prevention

Our flagship partnership model. An ongoing monthly audit cadence providing continuous transaction monitoring, monthly billing & warranty reconciliation, and rapid leakage arrest before month-end closures.

Key Inclusions:
  • Monthly audit of 100% of high-value ROs & 25% random sampling
  • Monthly DMS vs Physical Inventory discrepancy audit
  • Monthly OEM warranty rejection & refiling review
  • Monthly Executive MD Review & Exception Handover
  • Continuous CAPA tracking & staff accountability
Best Suited For: High-volume workshops, flagship metro branches, and dealerships aiming for near-zero revenue slippage.
MODEL 02

Quarterly Operational Audit

Periodic Health Check & Governance Review

A structured quarterly review examining rolling 90-day operational performance, identifying emerging leakage trends, and maintaining operational discipline across all after-sales departments.

Key Inclusions:
  • Quarterly deep-dive across all 10 audit practice modules
  • Physical count verification for Top 20% Fast/High-Value SKUs
  • Quarterly discount & manual override policy compliance
  • Gate security & non-invoiced vehicle exit checks
  • Quarterly Board Presentation with 90-day trend analysis
Best Suited For: Stable, mid-sized dealerships (1–3 locations) desiring independent periodic oversight.
POPULAR STARTING POINT
MODEL 03

One-Time 360° Diagnostic Audit

Complete Historical Baseline & Deep Investigation

An intensive forensic assessment covering 6 to 12 months of historical transactional data. Identifies historical leakages, uncovers uncollected dues, and delivers an immediate baseline scorecard.

Key Inclusions:
  • Comprehensive 360° audit of past 6–12 months records
  • 100% Physical parts inventory count & valuation
  • Complete unbilled parts, unbilled labour & gate slip audit
  • Detailed root cause analysis across all 10 service modules
  • Immediate 30-day unbilled revenue recovery plan
Best Suited For: First-time audits, dealership acquisitions, management restructuring, or pre-OEM audit preparation.
MODEL 04

Multi-Location Dealer Group Program

Multi-Branch Standardization & Group Governance

Designed for automotive groups operating 4 to 25+ workshops across multiple cities. Establishes standardized audit parameters, inter-branch benchmarking, and consolidated Group CFO reporting.

Key Inclusions:
  • Uniform audit methodology deployed across all group branches
  • Inter-branch revenue leakage & efficiency ranking index
  • Consolidated Group Executive Dashboard for Group Chairman/CFO
  • Branch-wise Exception Registers & local manager accountability
  • Centralized group SOP alignment & policy hardening
Best Suited For: Large automobile dealership conglomerates, multi-state dealer networks, and multi-brand groups.

Our 4-Step Engagement Onboarding Protocol

Starting an audit engagement is structured, transparent, and completely non-disruptive to your daily workshop floor operations.

1

Confidential Scoping & NDA

We execute a mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). We review your brand portfolio, workshop count, and specific focus areas to tailor the audit scope.

2

Read-Only Data Extraction

We configure read-only DMS reports and transactional data exports. Your staff continues servicing vehicles without hindrance or operational interruption.

3

Analytical Audit & Field Verification

Our audit engine runs multi-way reconciliations. Auditors conduct targeted physical sampling, security gate checks, and root-cause investigations.

4

Executive Handover & Action Plan

We present the Executive Dashboard and Exception Register to Dealer Principals and CFOs, handing over clear SOP updates and CAPA implementation targets.
