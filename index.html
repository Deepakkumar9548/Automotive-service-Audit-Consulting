Industry Realities

Common Dealership Challenges We Uncover & Resolve

In high-velocity automotive workshops, daily operational pressures often mask systemic revenue leakage, control failures, and accounting mismatches. We provide an independent lens to protect your margins.

Unbilled Labour & Spare Parts

Parts issued from store without job-card linkage, unbilled additional labour operations, and technician clocking mismatches that erode gross profit.

Unauthorized Discounts & Waivers

Discounts given beyond authorized delegation limits, undocumented manual overrides, and unbilled value-added services like washing and pick & drop.

Warranty Claims Disallowances

OEM debit notes on rejected claims, delayed IRN submissions, missing defective parts scrap entries, and aged claims exceeding warranty limitation windows.

Payment Reconciliation Differences

Cash, UPI, and payment gateway collections not tied to tax invoices, unmatched receipts, credit customer overruns, and unrecovered short payments.

Inventory & Physical Stock Gaps

Physical bin quantities diverging from DMS balances, negative stock adjustments, scrap pilferage, and capital trapped in slow/non-moving parts.

Gate-Out Security Control Failures

Vehicles released on manual gatepasses without verified tax invoices, late-night exits without management intimation, and missing gate log records.

Poor Workshop Productivity & TAT

Stalled work-in-progress (WIP), high vehicle turnaround time (TAT), bay idling, repeat repairs, and customer delivery delays reducing bay throughput.

Inaccurate MIS & Filtered Reporting

Manual spreadsheets masking operational realities, unverified KPI claims, and lack of cross-reconciled data for Dealer Principals and MDs.

Structured Execution

Our End-to-End Audit Approach

A rigorous, data-driven methodology that transforms raw dealership transactions into verified exceptions, quantified financial impact, and actionable management decisions.

01

Data Collection

Direct extraction of raw DMS tables, physical gate registers, store requisitions, and cashier records without disruption.

02

Data Validation

Deduplication, date normalization, sequential invoice auditing, and schema alignment across all branch datasets.

03

Transaction Testing

100% line-item verification of labor operation codes, standard flat rates, and parts prices against approved master matrices.

04

Reconciliation

Multi-way triangulations: Gate-Out vs Invoices (GOR), Store Issue Slips vs Billed Parts, and Bank Deposits vs Daybook.

05

Exception Identification

Flagging every unbilled repair, unapproved discount, manual gatepass exit, and unreconciled payment with exact row references.

06

Audit Reporting

Formulating the comprehensive audit pack: 1-page MD dashboard, branch comparisons, and quantified leakage annexures.

07

Management Review

Collaborative discussion with Dealer Principals, GMs, and Service Heads to validate operational realities and root causes.

08

Corrective Action

Formulating a time-bound CAPA roadmap with assigned departmental owners to immediately plug financial and process leaks.

09

Follow-up

Post-audit 30-day/60-day compliance reviews to confirm sustainable implementation of internal controls and zero leakage.
