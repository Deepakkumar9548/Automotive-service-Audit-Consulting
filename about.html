About Our Consulting & Audit Practice

Dedicated exclusively to the automotive after-sales domain. We provide independent operational verification, transaction reconciliations, and risk mitigation for automobile dealerships.

Our Signature Positioning
"We Don't Just Audit Operations. We Identify Revenue Leakage, Strengthen Internal Controls and Improve Dealership Profitability."
Who We Are

Specialized Automotive After-Sales Practitioners

Modern automobile dealerships face razor-thin margins on vehicle sales, making the after-sales service workshop and spare parts business the engine of dealership profitability. However, due to the high volume of daily repair orders, multi-party insurance claims, complex OEM warranty rules, and fast inventory turnover, after-sales operations frequently suffer from systemic revenue leakage and operational control breakdowns.

We are an independent B2B audit, risk management, and operational consulting company specializing exclusively in automotive workshops. We do not sell software, repair vehicles, or manage parts distribution; our sole focus is conducting thorough, objective, data-backed operational audits and transaction-level reconciliations that protect dealer principals and management from unnoticed losses.

Our Audit Philosophy

The Five Tenets of Our Audit Practice

Institutional operating principles governing every dealership engagement, ensuring non-negotiable objectivity, forensic depth, and leadership trust.

01

Strict Independence & Objectivity

We operate with total independence from dealership operations and internal workshop politics, delivering candid, unfiltered findings directly to executive leadership.

02

Evidence-Based Reconciliation Engine

No finding is reported on hearsay or assumption. Every observation is supported by cross-verified source records (Gate Registers, DMS Job Cards, Parts Issue Slips, and Bank Ledgers).

03

Root-Cause Focus (Beyond Blame)

Our goal is not to police staff, but to identify the broken systemic workflows, authorization gaps, or system settings that allowed the leakage to happen.

04

Non-Negotiable Data Confidentiality

Dealership financial information, customer records, and OEM contract details are safeguarded by binding non-disclosure agreements and enterprise-grade data isolation.

05

Action-Oriented CAPA Deliverables

Every audit report concludes with a quantified Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA) roadmap, complete with department owners and resolution timelines.

Delivery Model

Hybrid Audit Delivery: Remote Analytics + On-Site Verification

To deliver maximum audit rigor without inflating travel expenses or disrupting everyday workshop throughput, we operate a hybrid delivery model:

Phase 1: Remote Data Analytics Desk

Comprehensive data extraction, normalization, script-driven cross-reconciliations, anomaly detection, and population sampling across 15 workshop data streams.

Phase 2: Targeted Field Verification

Physical verification of scrap bins, high-value warranty returns, manual gatepass files, customer signatures, and surprise physical spare parts counts.
