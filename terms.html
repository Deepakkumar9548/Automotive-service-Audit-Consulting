Terms of Professional Engagement

Standard terms and operational guidelines governing our automotive after-sales audit, risk reviews, and consulting services.

Last Updated: November 2026 | Effective across all B2B Client Contracts

1. Nature of Consulting Services

We provide independent operational reviews, data reconciliations, internal control assessments, and management advisory services for automobile dealerships. Our company does not repair motor vehicles, sell automotive spare parts, operate physical workshop bays, or manage day-to-day dealership personnel. All findings, exception reports, and recommendations are delivered in an advisory capacity to support dealership executive management.

2. Source Data Integrity & Client Cooperation

The accuracy and completeness of our audit findings rely fundamentally upon the authenticity of the transactional data provided by the client dealership. The client agrees to:

  • Provide unedited, complete raw exports from the Dealership Management System (DMS), security gate logs, and cashier records.
  • Facilitate timely access for authorized auditors during scheduled on-site physical stock counts or security register reviews.
  • Participate in the formal 5-day Management Discussion window to clarify exceptions before final report issuance.

3. Evidence-Based Verification Standard

Every audit observation, financial leakage figure, or process exception is strictly cross-referenced against supporting records (DMS RO numbers, invoice IDs, parts codes, or gatepass numbers). Where records are absent or ambiguous, observations are classified as "Unverified / Subject to Confirmation" until formal dealership review.

4. Statutory & Regulatory Compliance

Our audits evaluate operational and procedural adherence. They do not constitute statutory financial audits under the Companies Act or formal GST tax representation. The dealership remains solely responsible for its statutory tax returns, e-invoicing compliance, and OEM franchise agreements.

5. Intellectual Property & Deliverables Ownership

Upon full settlement of professional engagement fees, the final delivered audit reports, executive summaries, and customized exception trackers become the exclusive property of the client dealership. Our proprietary audit testing scripts, standardized data inventory questionnaires, and calculation algorithms remain our firm's intellectual property.

6. Jurisdiction & Disputes

All commercial engagement agreements are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with an audit mandate shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in [Insert Commercial City / State, India].
