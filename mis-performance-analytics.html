MIS & Performance Analytics

Independent transformation of complex DMS databases into automated executive dashboards, labor absorption analytics, multi-branch benchmarking, and root cause insights.

Practice Overview

Replacing Filtered Reports with Independent Business Intelligence

In most automotive dealer groups, executive leadership relies on manual Excel MIS reports compiled by branch managers and service heads. These internal reports often suffer from survivor bias: unbilled ROs are omitted, technician idle time is glossed over, customer complaint spikes are reclassified, and warranty disallowances are delayed in recognition.

Our MIS & Performance Analytics Practice ingests raw, un-doctored DMS database extracts to engineer automated, tamper-proof executive dashboards. We deliver true labor-to-parts billing absorption ratios, technician productivity curves, branch-versus-branch performance rankings, and clear Root Cause Analysis (RCA) directly to the Managing Director and Board of Directors.

Strategic Imperative

Why Dealerships Need This Audit

Management often receives conflicting or manipulated reports from departmental heads. Department managers mask poor productivity with dummy job cards, backdated closures, and selective KPIs, leaving Dealer Principals blind to real workshop performance.

Common Ground Blind Spots Uncovered:
  • Departmental reports manually modified before submission to executive directors.
  • Backdated job cards closed on month-end to falsely inflate monthly vehicle intake.
  • Conflicting metrics between DMS operational reports and financial accounting software.
  • Lack of consolidated multi-location visibility for dealer group principals.
Audit Scope

What We Audit & Model

  • Daily, Weekly & Monthly MIS: Re-engineering automated operational MIS directly from raw transactional tables.
  • Labour vs Parts Revenue Mix: Tracking the high-margin labour absorption ratio across workshop locations.
  • Technician Utilization & Efficiency: Tracking billed clocking hours against paid technician shop attendance.
  • Bay Velocity & Turnaround Throughput: Profiling bay revenue per square foot and bay turnaround cycle times.
  • Multi-Branch Benchmarking: Normalizing KPIs across dealerships to identify top-performing and lagging branches.
  • Automated Executive Dashboards: Building 1-page MD summaries and real-time visual exception monitors.
  • Root Cause Analysis (RCA): Deploying Pareto analyses on recurring process breakdowns and revenue leakage sources.
  • Service Retention Funnel Analytics: Tracking drop-off rates across free service, paid service, and post-warranty cohorts.
Risk Exposure

Common MIS Flaws & Distortions

MIS Distortions Frequently Found in Dealerships:

  • Filtered Excel Spreadsheets: Branch managers deleting open/delayed job cards or cancelled invoices from monthly reporting spreadsheets.
  • Distorted Labour Absorption: Including free service reimbursements or parts margins in labor absorption figures to artificially inflate workshop health.
  • Under-Reported WIP Ageing: Closing repair orders on the 30th of the month and immediately re-opening new ROs on the 1st to hide vehicles stalled >60 days.
  • Unconsolidated Multi-Branch Metrics: Dealer Principals lacking an apples-to-apples comparison between sister branches operating on slightly different local DMS rules.

Procedure

Our Analytics & Modeling Procedure

1
Direct Data Ingestion

Ingest raw, unedited CSV/Excel dumps exported directly from the dealership database without human intermediation.

2
Data Cleaning & Standardization

Normalize service categories (Paid Service, Accidental, Running Repair, Free Service, Goodwill) across all dealership locations.

3
Metric Re-computation

Programmatically compute true operational KPIs (Labour Billing %, Cost per Bay Hour, Average Ticket Value, Parts-to-Labour ratio).

4
Inter-Branch Variance Testing

Benchmark sister locations against group averages, spotlighting branches with abnormally low labor billing or excessive discounts.

5
Automated Visual Synthesis

Output interactive, drill-down dashboards and concise 1-page executive briefs tailored for executive review.
