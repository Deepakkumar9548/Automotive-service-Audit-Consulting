Consulting Engagement Lifecycle

Our Institutional 15-Step Audit Protocol (Phase-Wise Execution)

Every dealership engagement follows our rigorous 4-stage consulting architecture, guaranteeing data integrity, peer-reviewed accuracy, and collaborative closure.

PHASE 01 INCEPTION, SCOPING & DATA INGESTION (STEPS 01 – 05)

Scoping, Governance Charter & DMS Data Extraction

Formal confidentiality agreements, branch scope definition, Lead Auditor resource deployment, and extraction of 100% census data with cryptographic completeness checks.

⏱ Days 1 – 3 📁 5 Milestone Artifacts
01

Step 1: Client Onboarding & NDA Execution

Lead Partner & Legal Counsel

Formal execution of mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA) and secure data governance charters. We establish direct communication channels with Dealer Principals, CEOs, and designated audit liaisons.

✓ Deliverable: Signed Engagement Letter & Confidentiality Charter Control: Legal Non-Disclosure Protection
02

Step 2: Audit Scope Finalisation

Audit Director & Dealer Principal

Definition of participating dealership branches, workshop bays, period of review (e.g. preceding quarter or rolling 12 months), specific audit modules, and custom management priority areas.

✓ Deliverable: Formal Audit Scope Document Control: Branch & Workstream Perimeter Alignment
03

Step 3: Audit Planning & Resource Allocation

Engagement Manager & Senior Lead Auditor

Development of the execution schedule, risk matrix calibration, assigning the Lead Automotive Auditor, MIS Analyst, and physical field audit teams across workshop locations.

✓ Deliverable: Master Audit Calendar & Resource Matrix Control: Risk Matrix & Manpower Allocation
04

Step 4: Dealership Data Collection & Inventory

MIS Data Analytics Team

Collection of standard DMS reports: Job Card Master, Parts Issue Slips, Tax Invoices, Security Gate Registers, Tech Biometric Clocking, Store GRNs, Counter Sales, RSA Registers, and Bank Statements.

✓ Deliverable: Data Ingestion Log & Completeness Checklist Control: 100% Census Ingestion (No Sampling)
05

Step 5: Data Cleaning & Integrity Validation

Data Engineering Desk

Scripted deduplication, date normalization, checking sequential invoice integrity, verifying VIN validity, and mapping custom dealership column headers to standard audit schemas.

✓ Deliverable: Validated Master Audit Dataset Control: Missing Gaps & Schema Verification
PHASE 02 FORENSIC TESTING & RECONCILIATION ENGINE (STEPS 06 – 09)

Automated Rule Execution, Cross-Triangulation & Quantification

Executing algorithm engines on 100% of transactions, cross-system triangulation between gate/store/accounts, isolating exceptions, and measuring exact rupee financial exposure.

⏱ Days 4 – 9 📁 4 Core Analytical Workpapers
06

Step 6: Transaction-Level Audit & Rule Testing

Automotive Audit Specialists

Executing automated rule engines on 100% of line items: Checking discount authorization caps, flat-rate labor compliance, pre-invoice vs final invoice consistency, and GST tax codes.

✓ Deliverable: Transaction Scrutiny Working Papers Control: DOA Thresholds & Rate Validation
07

Step 7: Cross-Reconciliation (Core Engine)

Forensic Triangulation Team

Multi-system triangulation: Security Gate Log vs DMS Invoices, Store Issue Slips vs RO Billed Parts, Counter OTC Parts & Lubricants vs Store Ledgers, Roadside Assistance (RSA) Towing Logs vs Inbound Gate Entries, Cashier Daybook vs Bank Credit Statements, and Claims Filed vs OEM Credit Notes.

✓ Deliverable: Three-Way Reconciliation Matrices Control: 3-Way Independent System Match
08

Step 8: Exception Identification & Logging

Lead Field Auditor

Isolating every non-compliant record, unbilled service, unauthorized gate-out, and unverified discount into a standardized Audit Observation Register with source references and transaction IDs.

✓ Deliverable: Audit Exception Log with Source Row IDs Control: Zero Ambiguity Evidence Tagging
09

Step 9: Financial Impact Assessment & Quantification

Senior Financial Analyst

Calculating the exact monetary value of uncovered leakage (in ₹ Lakhs): Net unbilled parts value, unauthorized discounts, lost labor hours, and OEM chargebacks. Strictly zero speculative estimates.

✓ Deliverable: Quantified Financial Exposure Ledger Control: Actual Rupee Impact Formulation
PHASE 03 QUALITY REVIEW, MANAGEMENT VALIDATION & REPORTING (STEPS 10 – 13)

Independent Peer Review, Floor Validation & Board Reporting

Mandatory dual-sign-off quality control, collaborative fact-check roundtables with General Managers, structured rebuttal handling, and authoritative board pack delivery.

⏱ Days 10 – 12 📁 Executive Board Deliverables
10

Step 10: Peer Quality Review (QC Desk)

Independent Senior Review Partner

Mandatory secondary verification. A Senior Audit Partner independently verifies every finding against the raw source evidence to guarantee zero false positives before client presentation.

✓ Deliverable: Quality Assurance Sign-Off Certificate Control: Zero False Positive Protocol
11

Step 11: Draft Audit Report Formulation

Reporting & Synthesis Desk

Compilation of the comprehensive Draft Audit Pack: Executive Summary, Location-Wise Comparative Breakdown, Workstream Findings, and Detailed Transaction Annexures.

✓ Deliverable: Draft Audit Report & Exception Annexure Control: Granular Row-Level Evidence Annexures
12

Step 12: Management Discussion & Factual Clarification

General Managers & Audit Team

Interactive review with General Managers and Branch Service Heads. Dealership staff are given a structured 5-day window to provide evidence-backed explanations or supporting vouchers.

✓ Deliverable: Management Response & Rebuttal Tracker Control: Fair Collaborative Resolution Window
13

Step 13: Final Audit Report Presentation

Lead Partner, Dealer Principal & Board

Delivery of the authoritative final report to the Dealer Principal and Board, incorporating validated dealership explanations and presenting the definitive leakage figure.

✓ Deliverable: Final Executive Report & MD Presentation Control: Definitive Certified Financial Ledger
PHASE 04 CAPA IMPLEMENTATION, MONITORING & AUDIT CLOSURE (STEPS 14 – 15)

Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA) Alignment & Institutional Closure

Transforming audit discoveries into sustainable internal controls: Assigning named departmental owners, tightening DMS authorization hierarchies, and 30/60-day remediation audits.

⏱ Days 13 – 15+ (Ongoing) 📁 Sustainable Control Governance
14

Step 14: Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA) Tracking

Dealership HODs & Internal Control Head

Structuring a time-bound CAPA plan assigning named dealership department owners to plug identified control gaps, update DMS authorizations, and enforce physical gate security.

✓ Deliverable: Time-Bound Dealership CAPA Matrix Control: Named HOD Ownership & Milestone SLAs
15

Step 15: Remediation Follow-up & Audit Closure

Quality Assurance Lead & Dealer Principal

Conducting a 30-day or 60-day post-audit compliance check to verify that corrective actions were implemented and that recurring revenue leakage has ceased.

✓ Deliverable: Audit Remediation Closure Certificate Control: Verified Recurrence Cessation
