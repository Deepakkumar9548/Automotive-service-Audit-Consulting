Audit Practices

Comprehensive Workshop & Financial Scrutiny

Choose an individual targeted audit module or engage our comprehensive 360-degree after-sales audit framework.

1. Workshop Operations Audit

End-to-end evaluation of vehicle floor flow, open repair orders, technician clocking, bay productivity, and stalled WIP aging.

  • Open Job Card Aging & Cancellation
  • Technician Skill Clocking vs SLA
  • Turnaround Time (TAT) Bottlenecks

2. Spare Parts & Inventory Audit

Reconciliation of physical stock against DMS quantities, issue slip tracking, scrap control, and non-moving inventory analysis.

  • Physical Count vs DMS Discrepancies
  • Floor Issue Slip vs Invoiced Parts
  • Slow & Dead Stock Ageing
View Service Details →

3. Service Billing & Revenue Leakage

Comprehensive transaction audit to stop unbilled labour, unauthorized customer discounts, and overlooked chargeable work.

  • Pre-Invoice vs Final Billing Integrity
  • Discount Delegation Matrix Audit
  • Uncharged Wash & Sub-₹100 Jobs
View Service Details →

4. Warranty & Insurance Audit

Protection against OEM debit chargebacks, insurance surveyor short-settlements, delayed IRN submissions, and aged claims.

  • OEM Warranty Debit Note Analysis
  • 180+ Days Aged Claims Recovery
  • Bodyshop Insurance Reconciliations
View Service Details →

5. Counter Sales Audit

Forensic scrutiny of retail parts sales, lubricants, batteries, tyres, accessories, unbilled OTC sales, and cashier cash/UPI settlements.

  • Retail Parts & Lubricants Reconciliation
  • Unbilled OTC Sales & Cash Skimming
  • Sales Return & Cancellation Scrutiny
View Service Details →

6. Roadside Assistance (RSA) Audit

Verification of roadside assistance registrations, third-party towing vendor bills, OEM reimbursement receivables, and duplicate claims.

  • Free vs Paid RSA Enrolment Verification
  • Towing Vendor Billing & Route GPS Checks
  • OEM Reimbursement Claims & Aging Reco
View Service Details →

7. Customer Experience Audit

Verification of real customer feedback data, service complaint closure timelines, repeat visit issues, and PSF integrity.

  • PSF & CSI Data Authenticity
  • Escalated Complaint Closure TAT
  • Customer Retention & Churn Trends
View Service Details →

8. Financial & Payment Audit

Robust reconciliation of daily workshop collections (cash, UPI, credit cards, bank transfers) against tax invoices and debtor ledgers.

  • Cash & UPI Bank Reconciliations
  • Customer Ledger Scrutiny
  • Credit Notes & Refund Controls
View Service Details →

9. Gate Out & Vehicle Control Audit

Physical security reconciliation linking security gate passes to settled DMS invoices to eradicate unauthorized deliveries.

  • Delivery Without Billing Detection
  • Manual Gatepass Disallowance
  • Security Gate Register Reconciliations
View Service Details →

10. MIS & Performance Analytics

Custom analytics converting disparate DMS records into transparent executive dashboards, labour ratios, and multi-branch rankings.

  • Automated Executive MD Summaries
  • Labour vs Parts Billing Ratios
  • Multi-Location Benchmarking

Deliverables

What Dealership Management Receives

Every audit engagement produces structured, executive-ready deliverables designed for rapid decision-making and operational remediation.

Deliverable Component Target Audience Key Contents & Structure Operational Purpose
1. Executive Summary & MD Dashboard Dealer Principal, MD, CEO High-level KPI scorecard, total revenue audited, leakage quantified in ₹ Lakhs, top 5 control failures. Strategic decision-making & board oversight
2. Detailed Exception Annexure General Manager, Service Head Transaction-level line item register linking each exception to exact Job Card, Invoice, Gate Register, or Parts Code. Investigation, employee clarification, and recovery
3. Process & Controls Gap Analysis Operations & Quality Team Evaluation of current workshop SOPs against OEM guidelines, authorization matrices, and security protocols. Systemic policy and workflow refinement
