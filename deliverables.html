Deliverable 03

Multi-Point 3-Way Reconciliation Statements

Cross-system mathematical verifications eliminating the cracks between DMS, Accounting, and Physical Inventory.

Audience: CFOs, Financial Controllers, Auditors
1. Inventory 3-Way Reco

DMS Stock Ledger ↔ Physical Floor Stock ↔ Financial Books (Tally/SAP).

Uncovers unposted GRNs, negative stock items, transit stock leakages, and obsolete stock provisioning.
2. Revenue & Cash Reco

DMS Invoiced Value ↔ Bank Credits / UPI POS ↔ General Ledger Cash Book.

Identifies delayed cash deposits, uncollected customer balances, POS fee miscalculations, and unallocated advance receipts.
3. Bulk Fluid & Oil Reco

Digital Meter Dispatches ↔ Job Card Line Items ↔ Barrel Barrel Dipstick Stock.

Calculates fluid slippage, dispensing calibration drift, and unbilled top-ups across fast-moving workshop liquids.
Deliverable 04

Warranty & Roadside Assistance (RSA) Claim Pack

Dedicated analysis protecting OEM warranty reimbursements and 3rd-party RSA recovery.

Audience: Warranty Managers & Works Managers

OEM Claim Rejection Root Cause Tree

Categorizes warranty claim rejections (late submission, invalid causal part pairing, missing pre-repair diagnostic logs, missing customer signature) with refiling opportunity analysis.

Failed Parts Scrap Audit

Verifies physical quarantine room failed parts against warranty register to prevent OEM audit clawbacks, failed part theft, or duplicate claim submissions.

RSA Vendor & Towing Invoice Audit

Cross-verifies 3rd-party flatbed towing invoices, GPS logs, extra kilometre surcharges, and customer eligibility vs RSA policy coverage.

Deliverable 05

Over-The-Counter (OTC) & Retail Parts Audit Pack

Rigorous controls for walk-in retail sales, fast-moving lubricants, batteries, tyres, and accessories.

Audience: Parts Managers & Counter Sales Leads
  • OTC Cash/UPI Reconciliation: Reconciliation of day-end cash register, QR code transaction hashes, and DMS tax invoices.
  • Scrap & Old Part Replacement Audit: Old battery scrap registers, warranty replacement credits, and scrap core deposit tracking.
  • Non-Moving & Dead Stock Ageing Analysis: Parts age categorization (>90 days, >180 days, >365 days) with active disposal/return to OEM strategies.
  • Discount Policy Compliance: Verification of OTC walk-in mechanic discounts, trade credit limits, and sales return credit notes.
Deliverable 06

Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA) Roadmap

The post-audit operational transformation plan ensuring permanent rectification of uncovered vulnerabilities.

Audience: All Operational Stakeholders
30-Day Recovery Sprint

Targeted execution to recover immediate unbilled receivables, reclaim rejected warranty claims, and rectify critical security gate vulnerabilities.

60-Day Process Hardening

Implementation of revised DMS access controls, discount approval thresholds, barcode scanning at stores, and staff SOP retraining.

90-Day Verification Re-Audit

Formal re-audit check to measure CAPA compliance rates, confirm revenue leakage arrest, and present verified improvement scorecards to the board.
